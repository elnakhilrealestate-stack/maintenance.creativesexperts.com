Requirements to consider

One-time inspection or repair for a specific issue

Short-term support for a defined project or operating period

Planned maintenance visits for agreed equipment

Multi-site maintenance scope after each location is assessed

How a project can be scoped

  1. List equipment types, quantities and site locations
  2. Describe current faults and maintenance history
  3. Agree visit frequency, covered work and exclusions
  4. Document response arrangements, spare-parts terms and contract duration

Installation, maintenance and support options

Depending on the site and existing equipment, enquiries can cover a new installation, an upgrade, a one-time maintenance visit, a short-term support agreement or a longer-term maintenance contract. The covered equipment, visit schedule, exclusions, spare-parts terms and any response targets must be agreed in writing before work begins.
